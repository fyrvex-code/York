York

York is a historic city in the north of England. It is famous for its beautiful streets, old buildings and fascinating past.

01 / 05 · INTRODUCTION
A city with a long past

History of York

York was founded by the Romans in AD 71. Later, it became an important Viking city called Jórvík. Today, visitors can still see many signs of its history.

02 / 05 · HISTORY
The city's famous landmark

York Minster

York Minster is a large and beautiful Gothic cathedral. It is famous for its amazing stained-glass windows and impressive architecture.

03 / 05 · LANDMARK
Medieval atmosphere

The Shambles

The Shambles is one of the most famous streets in York. It has narrow lanes and old timber-framed houses. Many small shops and cafés make it a popular place to visit.

04 / 05 · OLD TOWN
Why visit York?

A city to remember

York is a wonderful place to explore history, enjoy traditional English food and walk along the old city walls. It combines the charm of the past with modern life.
